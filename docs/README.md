# Notes

Working notes kept from the sessions that produced particular features:
what was investigated, what was decided, and what was deliberately left out.

`../CHANGELOG.md` is the record of what changed and is the file to read first.
These are the longer versions, kept because the reasoning behind a decision is
harder to reconstruct than the decision itself.

- `CLIENT_INVESTIGATION.md` — how a device's identity is assembled, and what
  the correlation does and does not prove.
- `INCIDENT_TIMELINE.md` — the unified event view across five log sources.
- `MONITORING_UPDATE.md` — dashboard and alerting work.
- `AUDIT_FIXES.md` — an audit pass and what came out of it.
- `OFFLINE_MAC_LOOKUP.md` — the bundled IEEE OUI index.
