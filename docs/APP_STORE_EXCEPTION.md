## Why it exists

Apple's store terms limit how recipients may use and share an app. The Free
Software Foundation's position is that those limits are additional restrictions
the GPL forbids, which is how GPL apps have been removed from the App Store
before.

The sole author can publish their own code anywhere regardless. Once other
people's code is in the tree, publishing it under Apple's terms needs their
permission too. Stating that permission here, and accepting contributions only
under it, keeps the official App Store build possible without a separate
agreement for each contributor.

This is not legal advice. It follows the pattern other GPL-licensed iOS
projects use; have it reviewed if the question ever becomes more than
theoretical.
